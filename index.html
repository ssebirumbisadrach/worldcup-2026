THE WORLD'S GAME

The FIFA World Cup is one of the most watched sporting events on the planet. National teams from around the world compete for the most prestigious prize in international football.

The 2026 tournament introduces an expanded format featuring 48 teams and is hosted across Canada, Mexico and the United States.
